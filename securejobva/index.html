Questions

The things people actually ask.

Will my VA work my hours?

Yes. Your seat is scheduled to your time zone — a standard business day, an evening block, or split coverage across the morning and night. You set the shift on the scoping call and can change it with a week of notice.

Is this shared or dedicated?

Dedicated. One person, working only for you, in your tools and your systems. Not rotating between five other clients, and not a pooled ticket queue.

What if the person is not working out?

Tell your account manager. We start a replacement search the same day and you are not billed for the overlap. No replacement fee, and no minimum you have to serve out first.

How do you handle passwords and customer data?

Every VA signs an NDA and a data-handling agreement before day one. We recommend a shared password manager rather than sending credentials directly, and we set access to the least level the role needs. You can revoke all of it yourself at any time.

Do I need my processes documented already?

No, and almost nobody does. Documenting them is part of onboarding week — we sit with you, write the SOPs from how you already work, and hand you the document at the end whether you stay with us or not.

What is the minimum commitment?

None. Billing is weekly and you can end it with two weeks of notice. We would rather earn the next week than lock you into a year.

Tell us what is eating your week.

Twenty minutes, no pitch deck. If a virtual assistant is not the right fix for your problem, we will say so on the call.
